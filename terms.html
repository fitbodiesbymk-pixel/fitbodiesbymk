The coaching service

Fitbodies by MK provides 1-on-1 online fitness coaching. The service is delivered remotely via WhatsApp (text and voice notes) and email. There are no live video sessions unless specifically agreed in writing.

Each coaching programme includes:

  • A personal workout programme tailored to your goals and situation
  • A weekly check-in to review progress and adjust the plan
  • General nutrition guidance (not full nutrition coaching)
  • WhatsApp access to MK for questions and support

Full nutrition coaching is available as an optional add-on at an additional monthly fee, and only when requested by the client.

Pricing and commitment

Coaching is billed monthly. The minimum commitment is three months. This allows enough time to establish habits, make measurable progress, and build a productive coaching relationship.

Pricing is agreed before the programme starts and confirmed in writing. Your agreed rate is locked in for the duration of your commitment. Price changes for new clients do not affect existing clients during their current term.

Payment

Payment is processed via Stripe or PayPal. The first payment is due before coaching begins. Subsequent payments are due monthly on the same date.

If a payment is missed, coaching will pause until the account is brought up to date. If payment is not received within 14 days of the due date, the coaching arrangement may be ended.

Cancellation

You may cancel at any time, but payments made are non-refundable. If you cancel before the three-month minimum is complete, the remaining balance for the minimum term is still due.

After the minimum term, you can end coaching at any time by giving 14 days' notice via WhatsApp or email. No payment is due after the notice period ends.

Communication and response times

Coaching is delivered asynchronously — there are no scheduled calls or live sessions. MK is based in Doha, Qatar (UTC+3, no daylight saving). Messages are answered during waking hours in that time zone.

You will receive a response to most messages within one working day. During planned breaks (notified in advance), responses may take longer.

Your responsibilities as a client

To get the most from coaching, you agree to:

  • Show up to weekly check-ins — if you miss two in a row without notice, coaching will pause

Health screening and medical advice

All new clients complete a PAR-Q (Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire) before starting. If the PAR-Q indicates a condition that requires medical clearance, coaching will not begin until you provide written clearance from a doctor.

Coaching is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you experience pain, injury, or a medical concern, stop training and consult a qualified medical professional. MK will always refer you to a doctor for anything medical — not attempt to treat it.
