How it works

Everything runs through WhatsApp.

No apps to download. No booking system. No complicated tech. Just a WhatsApp conversation — text or voice notes, whatever feels easier for you.

01

Your programme

Your training plan comes to you before your first week starts. MK builds it around your goals, your schedule, your equipment, and what you told him in your application. It's yours — not a template.

02

Weekly check-in

Once a week, you send MK a short check-in message. He reads it, looks at how your week went, and sends back any adjustments. The programme evolves with you — it's never static.

03

Questions and support

Between check-ins, you have direct WhatsApp access to MK. Text or voice note. Questions, concerns, technique doubts — anything. You won't be handed off to an assistant.

04

Nutrition guidance

General nutrition guidance is included — practical, sustainable, not a rigid meal plan. Full nutrition coaching (PN1) is available as an add-on if you want to go deeper.

Your weekly check-in

What to send each week.

The check-in is the engine of your programme. It's how MK stays in sync with what's actually happening in your life — not what he assumes. Keep it honest, not perfect.

Weekly check-in template — copy this to WhatsApp

1
How many workouts did you complete this week? (e.g. 3 out of 4)
2
How did the sessions feel? (energy, effort, anything that stood out)
3
Any aches, tightness, or things that didn't feel right?
4
How has your eating been? (no tracking needed — just a general sense)
5
How has your sleep and stress been this week?
6
Anything else on your mind — or anything you'd like to change?

Send it as a voice note if writing feels like effort. The questions are a guide, not a form — anything that covers those six areas is fine. Short and honest beats long and polished.

How to get the most from this

A few things that matter.

These aren't rules imposed on you — they're the things that separate the people who get real results from the ones who don't.

✅

Do the check-in, even if it's bad

A week where nothing went right is the most important week to check in. That's the information MK needs most. Silence doesn't protect you — it just means the programme can't adapt.

✅

Tell MK when something hurts

Not every ache needs to stop the programme — but MK needs to know about it. Never push through real pain. Say something. The programme can always be adjusted.

✅

Start simple

The first few weeks are about building the habit, not breaking records. Resist the urge to add extra sessions or go harder than the plan says. Consistency is the goal right now.

✅

Life gets in the way — say so

Travel, illness, a hard week at work — these are real. MK has coached through all of it. Let him know so the plan adjusts, rather than you silently falling behind.

✅

Focus on how you feel first

The scale is one signal among many. Energy, sleep, how your clothes fit, how you move — these matter just as much. Notice all of it, not just the number.

✅

Ask the obvious questions

If an exercise doesn't make sense, or you're not sure about a form cue, or you want to swap something — just ask. No question is too small. That's what the access is for.
